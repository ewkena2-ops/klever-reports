The brief

Melamine ran out at eleven and the factory lost three hours. Nobody told Getachew until the afternoon, and the store had flagged two days of cover on Tuesday.

Store and stockwants you
White 18mm melamine ran short and stopped production today. Total is down to two days of cover, hinges to four. Both were handed to Getachew this morning. Offcuts came back from the floor today and none were reissued. The shop is cutting fresh board for parts that stock offcuts could already cover.
